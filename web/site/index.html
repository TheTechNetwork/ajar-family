Plainly, before you sign up

  • Ajar is in early testing. You can create an account and set up a family today, but the device apps are still going through Apple and Microsoft review.
  • We record what your child asks to open — not everywhere they go. There is no browsing history in this product, because approving a request does not need one.
  • You'll confirm your email before the account exists. We send a code and nothing is created until you use it, so a typo means no account rather than a silent one you never hear from.
  • We do not buzz your phone yet. An ask reaches you in seconds while Ajar is open in a browser tab or on your phone. Lock-screen alerts are the next thing we are building — until they land, an ask waits for you rather than finding you.
  • Your child can see that Ajar is there. It is not hidden and it is not a monitoring tool. The whole design assumes they know, and asks them to ask.
